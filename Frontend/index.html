What Are Sleep Cycles ?

Sleep happens in cycles, each lasting about 90 minutes. A full night's rest consists of multiple cycles, moving through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep.


Why Waking Up in the Middle of a Cycle is Bad

If you wake up during deep sleep, you’ll feel groggy, tired, and unfocused. This is called sleep inertia, and it can ruin your morning.

tip : Try to wake up at the end of a cycle for the best wake-up!


The Best Number of Sleep Cycles

For the best rest, aim for 4 to 6 full cycles per night:

  • 3 cycles (4.5 hours) – Not ideal but better than nothing.
  • 4 cycles (6 hours) – Decent for a short night.
  • 5 cycles (7.5 hours) – Recommended for most people.
  • 6 cycles (9 hours) – Perfect if you need extra rest.
